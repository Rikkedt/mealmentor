Meal Mentor AI

Privacy policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

Meal Mentor AI helps you eat well – also while you taper off weight-loss medication. To do that, we process information about you, including health information. Here you can read what we store, why, and what rights you have.

Who is responsible?

Sable & Mons I/S, CVR (Danish company reg. no.) 46888499, Grenåvej 107D, 8240 Risskov, Denmark.
Contact: info@mealmentorai.com

What information do we process?

  • Account: your email address and, if provided by your login provider, your name.
  • What you log yourself: meals (calories, protein, fat), weight, sleep, training, evening check-ins, and your goals and settings.
  • Health information: e.g. weight, sleep, mood, which weight-loss medication you take, your dose and phase (up, stable, down). Menopause status, if you choose to share it.
  • Photos of food: sent for analysis when you scan, but not stored by us.
  • Subscription: whether you have an active subscription, purchase date, expiry and trial period. The payment itself and your card details are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play – we never see them.
  • Technical information: e.g. IP address and time, which our hosting uses to deliver the service and stop abuse.

Why, and on what legal basis?

  • To provide the app and your account (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) – contract).
  • To give you advice based on your health information. This only happens with your explicit consent, which you give when you sign up (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)). You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your data or writing to us.
  • To manage your subscription and give you access to what you have paid for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) – contract), and to comply with the Danish Bookkeeping Act (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • To protect the service against abuse, e.g. a daily limit on AI use (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) – legitimate interest).

We never sell your information and never use it for advertising.

Who processes data on our behalf?

  • Supabase – database and login. Your data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • Cloudflare – hosting of the app and the daily AI limit.
  • Anthropic – the AI (Claude) that analyses food photos and writes advice. When you use the AI, what is needed (e.g. the photo or a summary of your day) is sent to Anthropic. Anthropic may process data outside the EU; the transfer is based on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or another valid transfer mechanism.
  • RevenueCat – manages subscriptions across iPhone, Android and web. RevenueCat receives your user ID (a random ID, not your email) and information about your purchases from the App Store or Google Play, so the app can tell whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat processes data in the USA; the transfer is based on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • Apple and Google – when you buy the subscription in the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google are independent controllers for the payment under their own privacy policies.

We have, or are entering into, data processing agreements with them.

How long do we keep data?

For as long as you have an account. Purchase information is kept for 5 years after the end of the financial year, as required by the Danish Bookkeeping Act. If you delete your account under Profile → Account and data → Delete my account, the account and all your data are deleted immediately. You can also write to us, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Your rights

  • Access your data and get a copy (under Profile → Account and data → Advanced → Copy data).
  • Have data corrected or deleted (Delete all data or Delete my account in the app, or write to us).
  • Withdraw your consent.
  • Complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (datatilsynet.dk) or the data protection authority where you live.

Children

Meal Mentor AI is only for people aged 18 and over.

Not medical advice

The app gives advice about food and habits but does not replace your doctor. Always talk to your doctor before changing or stopping your medication.
